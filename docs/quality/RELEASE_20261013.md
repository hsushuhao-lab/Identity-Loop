## Mobile tap movement and fullscreen follow-up

Tapping a registered object approaches its visible hit point, stops at 1.5 m and aims at it; interaction remains an explicit center-button action. Existing wall collisions and floor support apply, and tap movement stops before skybridge portal bounds. It never opens a door, commits M9 or triggers the B2 one-way exit. Retapping replaces the destination.
