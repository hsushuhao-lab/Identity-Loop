# Mobile hospital: optional exploration increment

The production engine remains Vite/Three.js. Desktop and touch browsers share one game and run save. This is an incremental playable prototype, not the complete hospital or an AAA release. No native package, offline service worker, or measured physical-phone frame-rate acceptance is included.

## World mapping

Keep existing first-campus 3F administration/history, 4F wards/nursing station, 8F bridge and identity-only B2 unchanged. The proposed 6F skills center, broader ER, parking, morgue and remaining floors require later content and route review. `ward_service_annex` is a new 4F service annex reached behind the existing entrance storage-room door, not a replacement floor or elevator destination.

## Playable addition: 沒有風的驗收

The annex has a walkable vestibule, archive and low-voltage test room. Inspect the anonymous 1998 original or independently inspect the burnt relay and powered vent. Power and damper are physical separate interactions. The indicator can light while the paper strip remains still; open the damper and observe real fan/strip animation to record airflow. Archive unresolved facts or retain the original with a new observation receipt. Return immediately, revisit, or revise the local result. Four identities respond differently without naming personnel. A return receipt and patrol conversation reflect the investigation back in 4F; extension 708 gives an anonymous lead.

The test circuit never controls the hospital/B-Panel or establishes fire origin, culpability or identity. No outcome gates M1–M9 or writes identity evidence. B2 remains one-way and M9 retains one declaration. The excursion removes the mainline interaction binding only after assets are ready and rebinds the same beat on return. Cancel before readiness retains the current zone. A permanently available return control covers incomplete investigations. Reload returns to the saved mainline checkpoint, preserving local case records.

## Rendering and input

The anonymous equipment attendant has cloth grain, mask, articulated arms/legs, distance-driven gait, inspection pose and nearby-player orientation. Supported props gain rounded edges, readable source labels, wheelchair spokes/casters, local warm lighting and contact shading. Existing shared licensed models/PBR assets are reused; new geometry and canvas labels are generated in code.

Touch uses left movement, right look, central tap/550ms observation, journal, pinch and contextual continuation. Interaction opens after the synthesized click target is settled to prevent a new modal answer receiving the original touch. Performance/Quality/Ultra remain the existing presets and draw/triangle budgets. Vibration is optional and capability-detected; no control over system earpiece, brightness or gyro is claimed.

## Validation

`test-hospital-side-case.mjs` checks independent clue paths, persistence, revision history and four anonymous responses. `test-hospital-annex-browser.mjs` uses the production bundle, supported raycast approaches, real E/touch/button input, a controlled continuous walk through both rooms, exact mainline-save/beat comparisons, return/revisit/reload and emergency-entry protection. `test-mobile-hospital-browser.mjs` retains the existing desktop/touch/elevator/fallback regression. QA uses browser emulation and records render counters, not physical-phone FPS.

Further work includes authored/rigged characters, full hospital systems, real multi-camera CCTV, richer NPC schedules and threat behavior, actual Android/iOS device acceptance, native packaging assessment and offline/version migration design. Preserve the current engine until measurements justify another architecture.
